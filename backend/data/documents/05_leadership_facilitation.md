## Purpose

Cadre combines technical experience, behavioral science, and executive coaching to help leaders and teams adopt AI. Sessions address resistance, employee concerns, leadership alignment, and practical opportunities in the client's operations.

## Session structure

The published mix is 30% teaching, 30% interaction, and 40% application to business problems. Activities include collaborative problem solving and selecting a small set of relevant opportunities with next steps.

## Formats

The page lists a two-day leadership intensive, a one-day workshop, a half-day executive session, and a one-hour virtual kickoff. These are different facilitation formats, not a promise that every engagement includes all four.
