## Engagement and outputs

Cadre describes its AI Transformation Intensive as a 45-day strategy engagement developed jointly with client leadership.

The published sequence is:

1. Kickoff to align participants and deliverables.
2. AI Maturity Index to understand current adoption, employee concerns, enthusiasm, and learning preferences.
3. A full-day workshop examining departmental workflows.
4. A prioritized use-case library separating immediate opportunities from later work.
5. A three-year vision for the organization.
6. A twelve-month roadmap paced for the organization's capacity.

## Scope of the timeline

The 45-day description covers this strategy engagement. It does not establish a universal deadline for implementing every identified solution. The reviewed page does not provide a fixed engagement price.
