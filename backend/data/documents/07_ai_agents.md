## Types of solutions

Cadre's agent page distinguishes three levels:

- Prompts and assistants for repeatable daily work.
- Voice agents for intake, qualification, support, and routing.
- More capable agents that plan and act across tools with guardrails and human oversight.

## Published examples

The catalog includes a Brand Guardian for marketing consistency, an RFP Response Compiler, an Ops Dispatcher, and a Market Research Analyst. These illustrate different departmental uses.

## Interpretation

The catalog describes solutions and examples. It does not establish that every named agent is a downloadable product, that a subscription includes every agent, or that this demonstration chatbot can execute their actions.
