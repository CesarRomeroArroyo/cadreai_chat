## Strategy process

Cadre describes identifying business opportunities, assessing existing solutions, implementing them, and extending successful work across departments. Prioritization considers potential business impact and feasibility.

## Eight transformation pillars

The strategy page covers eight areas, paraphrased here:

1. An accountable, dedicated AI team.
2. A shared organizational AI platform.
3. Culture, policies, and adoption.
4. Connected systems and usable APIs.
5. Data quality and accessibility.
6. Agent preparation, procedures, and oversight.
7. Department-level workflow assessment.
8. A longer-term, three-year AI vision.

The framework connects organizational preparation with implementation rather than treating AI adoption as software purchasing alone.
