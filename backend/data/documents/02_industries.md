## Industries listed by Cadre AI

- Professional services: delivery automation and more time for specialist work.
- Private equity: deal sourcing, diligence, and portfolio improvement.
- Real estate: property analysis and lead qualification.
- Financial services: onboarding and compliance workflows.
- Mortgage and lending: underwriting and approval workflows.
- Construction: estimating quantities and monitoring projects.
- Retail and e-commerce: personalization and inventory operations.
- Manufacturing and logistics: equipment downtime, inventory, and fulfillment.
- Hospitality: guest service and demand planning.

## Other industries

The industry directory invites companies whose sector is not listed to discuss their needs. An absent industry is not evidence that Cadre refuses that sector.

These are published areas of application, not guarantees of project results or regulatory compliance.
