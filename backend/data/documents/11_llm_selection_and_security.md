## Model selection

Cadre describes selecting models for the client's use cases and establishing a shared AI platform. Its strategy discussion references ChatGPT, Copilot, and Claude as examples, without prescribing one platform for every organization.

## Security positioning

Cadre promotes controlled organizational AI use to reduce employees' disclosure of company information through personal accounts. Its page states an aim of preventing client data from being used for model training.

## Evidence boundary

These are Cadre's public service claims. The reviewed material does not establish contract-specific retention, hosting regions, certifications, or the security properties of this demonstration chatbot.
