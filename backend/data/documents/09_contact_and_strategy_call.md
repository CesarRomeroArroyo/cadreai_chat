## Contact options

Cadre's contact page publishes:

- Web form: https://cadre.ai/contact
- Support email: hello@gocadre.ai
- Telephone: (619) 324-3223

The form asks for name, email, subject, and message. The website's strategy-conversation links lead to this contact page.

## Requesting a conversation

The published contact options provide a way to ask about an engagement or request assistance. Reviewing this page did not establish live appointment availability, a guaranteed response time, or a completed reservation.

A chatbot that only shares these options has not submitted the form, sent an email, or booked an appointment.
