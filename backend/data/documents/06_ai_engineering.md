## Engineering approach

Cadre starts by identifying business impact, manual effort, and information trapped in disconnected systems. It then chooses between available products, workflow automation, and custom agents.

## Example capabilities

Published examples include data entry, document routing, email triage, report preparation, and customer inquiry handling. Integration work connects APIs and coordinates actions across existing tools. AI can support content-based routing and extraction from unstructured information.

## Custom development

Complex, repeated processes involving several steps may justify a custom agent. The page emphasizes selecting an approach appropriate to the problem rather than building custom software for every request.
