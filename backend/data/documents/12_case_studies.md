## Supplier confirmation automation

Cadre reports 220 hours saved monthly in an anonymized manufacturing and logistics case. The workflow extracts supplier information, checks NetSuite records, and sends exceptions for human review.

## Housing booking visibility

An anonymized hospitality case reports annual savings of $420,000. Cadre describes a CRM-connected dashboard that helps avoid conflicting same-day turnovers.

## Loan Intelligence Assistant

An anonymized mortgage and lending case reports 2,500 hours saved annually. The assistant provides access to lending guidelines and supports workflows involving loan tools.

## Evidence boundary

These outcomes are reported by Cadre, not independently audited in this corpus. The client organizations are not disclosed in these entries. Results do not establish prices, guaranteed savings, or expected outcomes for another customer.
