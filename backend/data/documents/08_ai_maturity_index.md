## Purpose

Cadre's homepage describes the AI Maturity Index as an assessment across eight transformation pillars, with grades, explanations, and improvement guidance.

## Public assessment entry

The public starting page is https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index. It advertises a free assessment taking about ten minutes for an individual and their team.

The initial form requests company name, company website, personal name, and work email. It states that a six-digit email code is used for verification.

## Verification boundary

Only the public entry page was reviewed. No form was submitted, email code requested, or assessment completed. The full questionnaire, grading formula, and individual results were not inspected.
