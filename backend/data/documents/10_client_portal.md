## Published purpose

Cadre's homepage describes a centralized client portal for tracking tools, agents, training, and results.

## Observed link destination

On the review date, the homepage's results-portal link opened https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index, a public assessment entry page. This destination was not verified as a client-dashboard sign-in page.

## Access limitation

The private dashboard, invitation process, password recovery, and account-specific access were not inspected. The base portal URL could not be inspected with the research tool. That limitation does not prove the service is unavailable.

This corpus contains no private client results or account credentials.
